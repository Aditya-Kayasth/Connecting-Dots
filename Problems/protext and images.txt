## 🇮🇳 Indian NGO Problem Statements

### 🌾 Rural Agriculture / शेती
**"Digital Market Access for Marginal Farmers in Vidarbha"**
> Small and marginal farmers in Vidarbha (Maharashtra) with less than 2 acres cannot access online mandi prices or sell directly to buyers. They are forced to sell through middlemen at 30–40% below market rate. We need a simple SMS/WhatsApp based tool that sends daily Nagpur mandi prices and connects farmers with FPOs (Farmer Producer Organizations).

---

### 🏥 Healthcare / आरोग्य
**"Maternal Health Tracking in Tribal Blocks of Palghar"**
> ASHA workers in Palghar's tribal belt track pregnant women using paper registers that get lost during monsoon. Over 60% of high-risk pregnancies are identified too late for intervention. We need an offline-first Android app for ASHA workers to record and flag high-risk cases that syncs when connectivity returns.

---

### 📚 Education / शिक्षण
**"First-Generation Learners Dropping Out After Class 8 in Rural Pune"**
> Over 40% of first-generation learners in our villages near Baramati drop out after Class 8 because parents cannot help with homework, and affordable tuition is unavailable. We need a peer-learning matching platform where college students in Pune can do 30-min video sessions with these students for free academic credit.

---

### ♿ Disability / दिव्यांग
**"Accessible Job Portal for Persons with Visual Impairment in Maharashtra"**
> Existing job portals (Naukri, LinkedIn) are not screen-reader compatible. Our 800+ registered blind and low-vision members across Nashik and Aurangabad cannot independently apply for jobs. We need a NVDA/JAWS-compatible job listing and application tool in Marathi and Hindi.

---

### 💧 Water / पाणी
**"Real-Time Handpump Failure Reporting in Marathwada"**
> 1,200 handpumps across 3 talukas in Osmanabad have no maintenance tracking. When a pump fails, it takes 15–30 days for the Gram Panchayat to report and get it fixed. We need a simple toll-free IVR + web dashboard for gram sevaks to report failures that auto-alerts the district water authority.

---

### 👩 Women Empowerment / महिला सशक्तीकरण
**"Digital Literacy for SHG Members to Access Government Schemes"**
> Our 2,000 Self Help Group members across Solapur district cannot independently access Jan Dhan, PM Ujjwala, or Ladki Bahin scheme portals because all government sites are in English with no guidance. We need a Marathi-language step-by-step video + chatbot guide for the 5 most important schemes.
